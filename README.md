**Senior software developer with 30+ years of experience.** I started on mainframes and never stopped learning. Today I develop complex COBOL systems at Banco do Brasil and, alongside that, I'm building a startup that creates value for the Brazilian music market through mobile and web technology.

> *"Legacy systems taught me stability. Modern tech taught me speed. I build with both."*

- 💼 30+ years in banking, government and enterprise systems
- 💾 Mainframe: COBOL, JCL, DB2, CICS
- 📱 Founder of **Bora**, the Michelin Guide of nightlife: if it's on Bora, it's worth your night. A project I envisioned for years while working with Brazil's top sertanejo (Brazilian country music) artists, and finally brought to life in 2025.
- 🧭 At Bora, I built the product vision, architecture, security, design and art direction, directing Claude Code to speed up parts of the process. I also had partners who helped steer the business at key moments.
- 🌱 Recent challenges I tackled at Bora: automated tax invoicing under Brazil's new tax reform (IBS/CBS), split payments between the platform and venues, and real-time distance and route calculation with geolocation.
- 🇧🇷 Based in Brazil, open to remote and international opportunities
